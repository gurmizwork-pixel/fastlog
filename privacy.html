Privacy Policy

FastLog – Fasting Timer  |  Solstice Apps  |  Effective: June 2025

Overview

FastLog is designed to respect your privacy completely. All data you enter stays on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • No account or login is required
  • No personal information is collected
  • No fasting data, history, or health information leaves your device
  • No location data is collected or used
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs are embedded in the app

Data Stored Locally

FastLog stores the following data exclusively on your device using your browser's localStorage:

  • Your selected fasting plan and any custom settings
  • Your fasting history and streak data
  • App preferences (time format, notification settings)
  • Whether you have purchased the "Remove Ads" feature

This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to Solstice Apps or any third party.

Advertising

The free version of FastLog displays banner advertisements. Advertisements are served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain data as described in Google's Privacy Policy. You can remove all ads with a one-time in-app purchase.

In-App Purchases

The "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by Google Play Billing. Solstice Apps does not receive or store any payment information. Google's own privacy policy governs the payment transaction.

Children

FastLog is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes in a meaningful way, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
